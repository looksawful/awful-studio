# Device visual acceptance

Language for reviewing device mockups and their screen artwork.

## Language

**Visible Dynamic Island**:
The black front silhouette visible on the display, with position and outline judged against the product reference.
_Avoid_: hardware keepout, front-camera center.

**Front hardware keepout**:
The engineering region reserved for the front camera and forward-facing sensors; its datum is independent of the visible Dynamic Island.
_Avoid_: visible island silhouette.

**Screen artwork**:
Replaceable display imagery. A cleaned screen plate contains the artwork without a duplicated device island.
_Avoid_: physical display assembly.

**Review candidate**:
A specific unapproved asset presented for visual judgment.
_Avoid_: accepted baseline, final delivery.

**Human Gate**:
The owner's explicit visual approval or rejection of an identified review candidate.
_Avoid_: automated test pass, agent visual review.
